Why Can’t we the people of these United States design A Government that continuouslyImproves itself?

Not “Why can’t our government improve?” in the sense of what is literally wrong with it. That is a very valid question, and We the People deserve an answer.

Not “Why can’t any government improve?” in the sense of why can’t any of the governments on Earth improve. That too is a very valid question that deserves an answer.

But in the “Why can’t we the people of these United States design a government that continuously improves itself?” sense of the phrase.

These aren’t excuses, and they aren’t personal accusations. Humans are imperfect. These are reasons why our government, the Government of the United States of America, can’t function when operated by people, human as they may be.

If you’re a politician, or you work in government…

…and you have answers

I, along with my fellow citizens, would like to hear them. Our government has had more than two centuries to work this out, and the problems have only grown. I imagine other people would like to hear your answers as well, so I’ll post them as I receive them.

Answers are accepted only from official government email addresses. Each address is confirmed before its answer is posted, and answers are posted without names or email addresses.

Thank you. I’ve got your answer.

Many people point to the people in charge, and they may be right. I’ve been asking a different question.

Picture an airliner that hasn’t been maintained in years. We keep choosing new pilots from the cabin, and smoke keeps coming from the engines. A new pilot doesn’t fix the plane.

Governance science is the work of fixing the plane: designing laws around what they are meant to achieve, checking whether they achieve it, and keeping a record voters can use.

Functional government. Under construction.
