What if the people aren't the problem?

Not the voters. Not even the politicians.

Most of us see government through politics. Some left. Some right. For most of us, it's a lot of "bleh": failures are met with plenty of finger-pointing, very little action, and even less improvement.

Isn't that just how it works? Politics is about policy: what our laws should say.

But underneath the policy there's a layer almost nobody looks at: the hows. How laws get made, and how laws are written.

A law usually spells out what to do. It rarely says what doing it should accomplish. So when a law goes wrong, there's no way to tell whether it worked.

That's our focus.
